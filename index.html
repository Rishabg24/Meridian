[ reason for doing so ]

Numbers alone convince no one, and they save no one. Every dataset sits on top of a question someone was afraid to ask aloud — who gets counted, who gets treated, who gets left outside the sampling frame. The work is not finished when the p-value is. It is finished when a ministry changes a policy, a clinic changes a protocol, or a community receives the resource the data said it needed.
